Introduction

TCP/IP stands for Transmission Control Protocol and Internet Protocol. It is the main communication protocol used by the Internet. Every time you open a website, send a message, or watch videos online, TCP/IP helps your device communicate with servers.

How TCP/IP Works

TCP/IP works by dividing communication into smaller packets. These packets travel through networks and are rebuilt at the destination.

TCP/IP Layers

  • Application Layer
  • Transport Layer
  • Internet Layer
  • Network Access Layer
